# Notice

This repository builds Hanpan's web editions of two open-source apps:

- **AI 포토** (AI Photo), based on [PhotoCraft](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft)
- **AI 일러스트** (AI Illustration), based on [VectorCraft](https://github.com/storytold/vectorcraft)

Both are by the ArtCraft team and its contributors and are dual-licensed MIT or Apache-2.0.
This repository holds only our patches and build scripts, under the same terms (see
`LICENSE-MIT`, `LICENSE-APACHE`); the upstream source is fetched at a pinned commit when building.

The builds are modified versions. They are not made, sponsored or endorsed by the ArtCraft team,
and the ArtCraft name and marks are removed from them as the upstream brand terms
(`docs/brand/LICENSE-brand.txt` in each upstream repository) require for modified versions.
Each build ships the upstream `LICENSE-*`, `NOTICE`, `ATTRIBUTION.md`, the licences of the fonts it
carries and `HANPAN-CHANGES.md`, the list of what we changed.
